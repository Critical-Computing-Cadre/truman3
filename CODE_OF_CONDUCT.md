# Code of conduct

Truman is research software, used and built by researchers, students, and developers at different stages of their careers. We want it to be a project where any of them can ask a question, report a problem, or propose a change, and be treated well.

## The standard

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Its text is the standard here. In short:

- **Be respectful** of people, their time, and their experience.
- **Give and take criticism of work,** and not of people.
- **No harassment,** insults, or unwelcome attention of any kind, in public or in private.
- **Don't share someone's private information** without their say-so.

## Where it applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests, and discussions, and anywhere someone is speaking for the project.

## Reporting

Tell the maintainer, privately, at **support@truman-research.org**. Say what happened, where, and when, with links if you have them. Reports are read by a person, kept confidential, and answered.

If the report is about the maintainer, say so, and ask for it to be passed to another member of the team at the Critical Computing Cadre.

## What happens

The maintainer decides what to do, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: from a private word, through a warning, to removal from the project's spaces.
