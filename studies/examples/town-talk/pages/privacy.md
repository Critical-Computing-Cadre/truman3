# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: January 2026_

This policy explains what Commons collects and how we use it.

## What we collect

- **What you give us:** your name, username, profile picture, and what you post, like, comment, and share.
- **How you use Commons:** what you look at, for how long, and what you do, so we can show you things you'll find interesting.
- **Your device:** your browser and device type.

## How we use it

To run Commons, to choose what appears in your feed, to keep Commons safe, and to improve it. We don't sell your personal information.

## Who sees what

What you post can be seen by other people on Commons. Your messages are seen only by the people you send them to.

## Keeping it safe

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no service is completely secure.

## Your choices

You can change your profile at any time, and ask us to delete your account by writing to privacy@commons.example.
