# Consent

This is a reference study for testing. Replace this with the consent text your IRB approved.

In this study, you'll use a community forum with other participants. **Some of the people you meet
are other real participants, taking part at the same time as you, and some are part of the study.**
What you write can be seen by the others in your group. Please don't share anything that identifies
you.

A study like this one shows people being rude to each other, so its consent and debriefing need to
say so.
