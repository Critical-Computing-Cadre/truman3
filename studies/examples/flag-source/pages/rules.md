# EatSnap.Love Community Rules

We're glad you're here to eat, snap, and love along with us! These rules keep EatSnap.Love
friendly. If you see anything that breaks them, **flag it** so our moderators can take a look.

## No bullying or harassment

Zero tolerance. Don't insult, threaten, or pile on anyone, about their food or about them.

## Food posts only

EatSnap.Love is for food: what you cooked, what you ate, and where. Keep posts and pictures about
food.

## Be kind

Disagree about pineapple on pizza all you like, but be kind to the person while you do.
