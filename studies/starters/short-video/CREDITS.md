# Where the sample videos come from

Every video here is cut from NASA footage, which is in the public domain. NASA's guidelines ask
that it be credited, and that it not be used to suggest NASA endorses anything. Each post names the
source in its text. The accounts that post them are made up, and aren't NASA.

What was changed in each: a stretch of 20 to 25 seconds was cut out, made tall for a phone (cropped,
or fitted on black), and its original sound was replaced with a soft chord. NASA's videos sometimes
use licensed music, so none of their sound is kept. `scripts/starter-videos-cut.swift` makes them,
and the line under each says how.
