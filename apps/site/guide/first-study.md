# Your first study

By the end of this page you'll have a small study with sample people and posts, and a link you can open to go through it as a participant would. Nobody else can reach it.

You need to be signed in to the admin app. On hosted Truman that's [app.truman-research.org/admin](https://app.truman-research.org/admin/). If your lab hasn't applied yet, do that first: see [Your lab on hosted Truman](/guide/your-lab). You can do everything on this page while your lab waits for approval.

## 1. Start the study

1. On the **Studies** page, press **New study**.
2. Fill in **Title**. Only researchers see it.
3. Fill in **Link name**, using lowercase letters, numbers, dashes, or underscores. Participants join at `/join/` followed by this name. It can't change later, so choose something a participant could see without learning what the study is about.
4. Under **What kind of platform is it?**, pick a card. Each shows a small sample of its posts. If you're unsure, open **Help me decide**. The kind can't change once the study starts.
5. Under **Where do you want to start?**, choose **Sample content** for the kind you picked. It copies in made-up people, posts, and pictures that you can change or delete.
6. Under **How are people assigned to conditions?**, choose **Uniform** or **Blocked**. Blocked keeps the group sizes close together. Either is fine for now.
7. Leave **Draft from a brief** empty, and press **Start the study**.

You land in the builder, looking at the feed as a participant would see it.

## 2. Change something

1. Click the text of any post and type. It saves when you click away. The top of the page says **All changes saved** when it has.
2. Under a post, open **Details** to change who wrote it, when it appears, and how many likes it has.
3. Move the slider at the top to see the feed at a later time in the study.
4. Press **Cast** to see the people. Press **Add a person** to add one, then **Back to the feed**.
5. Use **New post here** to write a post for someone in the cast.

Made a mistake? Open **Recent changes** and press **Undo**.

## 3. Write the consent page

1. Press **Settings**.
2. Scroll to **Study flow**. The first step is **Consent**, with a box of **Page text**.
3. Replace the placeholder with a sentence or two for now. Before a real launch, this is where the text your IRB approved goes.
4. Press **Save changes** on that card.

## 4. Save a version

A draft is where you work. A version is a frozen copy that participants can be given.

1. Go **Back to the feed** and press **Save as new version**.
2. Read what it tells you. If anything needs fixing first, it's listed there.
3. Press **Save version**.

Participants don't see a saved version until you launch it. You're taken back to the **Studies** page, where the version is listed under your study.

## 5. Go through it yourself

1. On the **Studies** page, find **Version 1** under your study.
2. Pick a condition in the dropdown beside **Try as a participant**, then press the button.
3. Press **Open as a test participant**. A new tab opens on the study.
4. Go through it: agree to the consent page, make a profile, and scroll. Like something. Write a comment.

That link is the one to send yourself. You're a test participant, so nothing you do ends up in the study's data.

## What to do next

- Press **Edit a copy of version 1** on the Studies page to go back to building. Saving again makes version 2.
- Add a second condition and limit a comment to it. See [Conditions](/guide/conditions).
- Make someone in the cast reply to what the participant writes. See [Making things happen](/guide/beats).
- When the study is ready for real people, see [Running a study](/guide/running). A lab on hosted Truman has to be approved before it can launch.

If you'd like to start from spreadsheets or from a folder of files, see [Posts and the cast](/guide/posts-and-cast) and [Studies as files](/developers/study-files).
