# Library

These are studies that labs have run on Truman and chosen to share with everyone. Each one is a replication bundle: the site as participants saw it, with its people, posts, conditions, and what happened when. There are no participants or data in any of them.

Download one to look through it, or to rerun it. To load it into Truman, sign in, press **New study**, and choose **From a replication bundle**. You get the whole study as a draft of your own. Its consent page starts with placeholder text, since yours has to be the wording your own ethics board approved.

Check each study's license before you use it, and cite the paper it came with.

To publish one of yours, see [Your data](/guide/data#publish-it-to-the-library).
